Vank dictionary attribution

The bundled seed vocabulary is project-owned curated data. For dictionaries
rebuilt with Wiktionary records, the imported definitions and adapted dataset
are made available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Wiktionary contributors: https://en.wiktionary.org/
Structured extraction: Wiktextract / Kaikki, Tatu Ylonen and contributors
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Armenian/
https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract

The builder intermediates retain source URLs and source names. The runtime
words.json omits build-only provenance; the builder emits an entry-by-entry
ATTRIBUTION.txt beside its dictionary output.

Changes: filtering, uppercase normalization, merging curated vocabulary,
deterministic Eastern Armenian learner readings, structural metadata, and
optional AI semantic metadata/manual corrections. No example quotations,
audio, or images are imported.
